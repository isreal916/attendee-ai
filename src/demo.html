Attie: an HR assistant that remembers you

Attie answers employees' HR questions from live HR records, and remembers what they share about how they work, using Walrus Memory. Pick an employee of Lumen Logistics, a made-up demo company, tell Attie something about yourself, then start a new chat and see it come back.

Shared demo: everyone who picks the same employee shares their memories. Please don't type real personal information.

Every memory is stored as an encrypted blob on Walrus (__NETWORK__); its link opens the blob on Walruscan. HR figures always come from the HR record and are never stored. Code: github.com/isreal916/attendee-ai
